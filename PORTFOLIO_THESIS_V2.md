## Evidence-derived thesis

The Schedule 13D result changes the search thesis, not its frozen rules or
adverse disposition. Its representative sample had 49 closed signals, positive
mean expectancy, positive chronological halves, and zero violations, but it
failed profit factor, bootstrap lower expectancy, performance without the five
best trades, and 20-bps stressed profit factor. This exact five-session variant
therefore shows why event salience and a positive sample mean do not establish a
broad, scalable residual edge.

For subsequent event-driven families, preregistration must identify why demand
or price discovery should continue after the public event timestamp. Positive
information content alone is insufficient. Development's bootstrap,
without-five-best, and cost-stress gates are core tests of that causal claim;
they are not secondary diagnostics and remain numerically unchanged.

The accelerated-share-repurchase theme is narrowed before any candidate or
outcome is frozen. An eligible capacity event must be an executed accelerated
repurchase agreement with point-in-time evidence of committed notional and
delivery or settlement mechanics. A board authorization, ordinary open-market
repurchase announcement, or generic statement of repurchase intent does not
establish the continuing-flow mechanism. The capacity preflight must measure
whether this stricter causal class can still support at least 30 Stage 0 and 50
total historical signals before a strategy variant is preregistered.

Executability is part of the thesis. If information known at the proposed entry
cannot produce a positive, protectable frozen stop, the event is a missed entry.
It cannot receive an alternative stop, substitute date, or parameter repair
after later prices are visible.
