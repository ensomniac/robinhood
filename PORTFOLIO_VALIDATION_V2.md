## Objective

Machine-earn `FIRST_PILOT_READY_LIVE_STARTED` for one exact long-equity or ETF
strategy version: `portfolio_maturity.py` must award `PILOT_READY`, one complete
controlled live execution must close and reconcile flat, and every repository,
privacy, broker-safety, capacity, lifecycle, and pushed-Git audit must pass.
`PILOT_READY` remains pre-live evidence, not a claim of live profitability.

## Superseding authority

Ryan explicitly authorizes up to three new mechanism families per ISO week.
All v1 evidence remains immutable and adverse. No failed variant may be repaired
on its evaluation corpus, relabeled, omitted from the trial record, or admitted
to maturity. No evidence, promotion, privacy, broker-safety, protection, loss,
drawdown, or portfolio-risk gate may be weakened.

The user authorization is recorded only through a content-addressed artifact
built by `portfolio_successor_activation.py`. Research activates only after
`portfolio_successor_activation_inspection.py` independently writes
`AUTHORIZED_READY`. Before that transition, candidate preregistration, provider
access, target outcomes, and broker actions remain forbidden.

## Discovery order

The initial theme order is:

1. Multi-asset ETF time-series momentum.
2. Schedule 13D activist continuation.
3. Accelerated-share-repurchase continuation.

Themes are not strategies. Before outcome access, each exact candidate must
pass an outcome-blind capacity preflight showing causal point-in-time inputs and
room for at least 30 Stage 0 signals and 50 total historical signals. Then its
rules, universe, dates, fills, exits, maximum five-session hold, 5/10/20-bps
cost grid, missing-data behavior, falsification criteria, and implementation
hashes must freeze and pass independent zero-result inspection.

Stage 0 still requires at least 30 signals, positive expectancy, profit factor
at least 1.10, maximum drawdown at most 8R, positive total R at 20 bps per side,
and zero violations. A failure retires the exact version immediately. No
parameter repair is allowed on that corpus.

## Promotion and live execution

Every Stage 0 survivor must retain its exact rules through representative
development, a chronologically separated untouched confirmation sample with a
five-session embargo, five complete prospective shadows, and independent
inspection. The minimum portfolio gates in `portfolio_config.toml` and
`PORTFOLIO_VALIDATION.md` remain authoritative; only
`portfolio_maturity.py` may award `PILOT_READY`.

Live execution remains impossible before `PILOT_READY`, a fresh
`FLAT_RECONCILED` account and order state, risk reconciliation, complete entry
and protection paths, and all broker/tool confirmations. The first-pilot goal
is complete only after one eligible live execution closes, all residual orders
are reconciled, the account is flat, all audits pass, and Git is clean and
pushed.
